Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CryptoMine Pro

How does CryptoMine Pro increase mining profitability?

CryptoMine Pro uses advanced AI algorithms to continuously optimize your mining settings based on real-time factors like Bitcoin price, network difficulty, electricity costs, and pool performance. This can increase profitability by 20-40% compared to standard mining software.

Is CryptoMine Pro compatible with my mining hardware?

Yes! CryptoMine Pro supports all major ASIC miners including Bitmain Antminer, MicroBT WhatsMiner, Canaan Avalon, and Innosilicon models. We also support GPU mining rigs with NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards.

How secure is my mining operation with CryptoMine Pro?

Security is our top priority. We use military-grade encryption, support 2FA authentication, and never store your private keys. All connections are encrypted with TLS 1.3, and we offer optional cold storage integration for maximum security.

Can I use CryptoMine Pro on multiple devices?

Absolutely! Your CryptoMine Pro account can be accessed from unlimited devices. Monitor your mining operation from your desktop, tablet, or smartphone with our responsive web interface and dedicated mobile apps.

What are the system requirements?

CryptoMine Pro is lightweight and runs on Windows 10/11, macOS 10.15+, and most Linux distributions. For the web interface, any modern browser works. Mobile apps require iOS 12+ or Android 8+. No special hardware requirements beyond your mining equipment.

How do I get started with CryptoMine Pro?

Getting started is easy! Sign up for a free account, download the software, connect your mining hardware, and start mining within minutes. Our setup wizard will guide you through the entire process step by step.
